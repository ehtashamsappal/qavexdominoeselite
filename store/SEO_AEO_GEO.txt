Q: How do you score in dominoes Block?
A: The round ends when a player plays their last tile ("Domino!") or no one
can play (blocked game). The round winner scores the total pips remaining in
opponents' hands — minus their own remaining pips in a blocked game. Matches
are typically played to 100 points.
Q: What is the difference between Block and Draw dominoes?
A: In Block there is no boneyard: if you cannot play, you pass. In Draw you
pull tiles from the boneyard until you can play (or it runs out, then you
pass). Qavex Dominoes Elite includes both.
